# Value statement

> **Draft for you to rewrite in your own words.** Everything below is accurate to the app as built; change the wording until it sounds like you.

**The Vegas Backroom** puts everything a sports bettor needs to make an informed decision in one place. Today, researching a single bet means jumping between sportsbook sites for odds, ESPN for injuries and news, stats sites for team and player trends, and a notes app to keep track of picks. The Vegas Backroom brings it all together for NFL and NBA games: odds from Hard Rock Bet and BetMGM side by side, the latest injuries and news, team and player stats, and live scores. Bettors can build straight bets, parlays, and same game parlays on a saved bet slip, see an estimated payout, get a warning when two picks can’t both win, copy an old slip as a starting point, and watch each pick turn “winning” or “losing” as the game plays out. The value is less time hunting across tabs and more confidence in every pick, because the research, the best price, and the bet slip all live on one screen. The app is a reference tool for adults 21 and over: no real bets are placed, no money changes hands, and it links to responsible-gambling help.
