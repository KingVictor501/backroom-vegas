# Value statement

> **Draft for you to rewrite in your own words.** Everything below is accurate to the app as built; change the wording until it sounds like you.

**The Vegas Backroom** is a reference tool for sports bettors who shop between sportsbooks. It puts odds from Hard Rock Bet, BetMGM, and Caesars side by side with the news, injuries, team and player stats, and live scores that bettors normally hunt for across many tabs, for NFL and NBA games. Bettors can build straight bets, parlays, and same game parlays on a saved bet slip, see an estimated payout, get a warning when two picks contradict each other, copy an old slip as a starting point, and watch each pick turn "winning" or "losing" as the game plays out. The problem it solves is that comparing prices, checking who is injured, and tracking a slip normally means juggling several sites and a notes app, and that makes it easy to miss a better line or build a slip that can't all come true. The value is one place where a bettor can see the best price, understand the matchup, and track their picks, with nobody placing a real bet or spending real money. The app is for reference only, for adults 21 and over, and links to responsible-gambling help.
